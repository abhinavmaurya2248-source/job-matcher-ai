How the scores work

Job Match Score

50% text similarity (TF-IDF + cosine similarity) + 50% skill match — the share of the job's recognised technical skills that also appear in your resume.

If the job description lists no recognised technical skills, skill match cannot be measured, so the text similarity is used as the score on its own.

ATS-style Resume Score

40% skill match + 35% keyword relevance (how many of the job's meaningful keywords your resume also uses) + 25% text similarity.

When the job description has no recognised technical skills, the skill part is not measurable, so its 40% weight is shared proportionally between the other two parts — about 58.3% keyword relevance + 41.7% text similarity — keeping their original ratio.

Both scores are calculated from your actual resume text and the job description you provide. They are estimates for guidance, not the scoring of any real ATS software.
